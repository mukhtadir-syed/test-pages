1 - Scope

Project Envelope

The initial implementation focuses on manage-billing customers in panels where direct payment exposure is highest. In-scope events are $create_order and $transaction for both direct payment and wallet withdrawals.

Panels: SuperSite, Optimus, CCB, Wipro Decisions: Accept, Block, Watch Hierarchy: Newfold → Reseller → Sub-reseller → Customer
2 - Phases
Phase 1 - current
  • Manage-billing scenarios prioritized for chargeback risk
  • Two event families wired first: order creation and payment transaction
  • Wallet deductions included so high-risk wallet behavior is visible
  • Delivery sequence designed for incremental operational readiness
Phase 2 - future expansion
  • Broaden to full OrderBox transaction coverage
  • Consider additional gateway scenarios and enrichment paths
  • Improve customer messaging and notification quality
  • Refine fraud automation once baseline event quality is stable

6 - Risks and Actions

Operational Load Risk

Customer-level blocking can spike fraud-ops review volume. Volume baseline and staffing assumptions need to be modeled before aggressive rule rollout.

Signal Quality Risk

Reseller and API-heavy paths have weaker browser fingerprinting. Additional headers and alternative device hints must be agreed and standardized.

Model Warm-Up Risk

Insufficient historical backfill can degrade early scoring quality. Minimum dataset contract with Sift is still pending and may delay confidence.
