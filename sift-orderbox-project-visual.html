1 - Summary

Project Summary

Newfold already uses Sift for retail transactions. This initiative extends coverage to OrderBox wholesale and reseller transactions, where payment and account hierarchies are structurally different.

Platform: OrderBox Primary Jira: EPMO-3220 Secondary Jira: SOFT-184794 Cadence: Weekly, Thursday 7pm IST
2 - Why This Matters

Risk and Financial Exposure

  • Manage-billing customers create direct chargeback liability.
  • ATO patterns include stolen-card wallet funding followed by domain registrations.
  • Scale spans BigRock, ResellerClub, SuperSite, and ExpressCart resellers.
  • Fraudulent registrations can drive ICANN compliance risk.

Why Retail Logic Is Not Enough

  • Wholesale relationships include reseller and sub-reseller layers.
  • Wallet funding and managed billing differ from direct retail checkout.
  • White-label brands require data modeling decisions per relationship type.
  • PM and engineering guidance is required for wholesale-specific behavior.
3 - Phased Approach

Phase 1 (Current)

  • Scope: Manage billing (PayPW) customers only.
  • Events: $create_order, $transaction (direct + wallet withdrawal).
  • Panels: SuperSite, Optimus, CCB Panel, Wipro Panel.
  • Timeline estimate: 1+ year for full implementation.

Phase 2 (Future)

  • Expand to all OrderBox transactions for broader pattern detection.
  • Add third-party gateway coverage (PayPal, Authorize.net).
  • Improve error messaging and customer notifications.

  • Asynchronous: 180 req/s.

Hierarchy Model

Relationship hierarchy: Newfold > Reseller > Sub-reseller > Customer. The actor can be reseller or end-customer depending on flow.

  • user_id: end-customer or reseller performing action.
  • merchant: reseller owning customer relationship.
  • Enables reseller-level fraud spike detection and cross-brand patterns.
