Crazy Domains · Premium Outbound · Pilot

A data-led way to give Sales better premium-domain conversations.

We start with customers who already bought premium domains, use EDS to find Sedo aftermarket matches, enrich the context, and turn it into a simple sales sheet that tells reps who to call, what to pitch, and how hard to push.

02

Why now

Three things make this a learn-fast pilot worth running with no engineering build and no new spend.

Customer base
Existing premium buyers
Crazy Domains already has thousands of customers who have bought premium-class domains from us. They are pre-qualified by purchase intent.
Inventory
Sedo aftermarket inventory
EDS already returns matching premium inventory from the Sedo aftermarket with live Buy Now prices. We do not need to source anything new.
Gap today
No automated outbound
Sales does not have a structured premium-outbound list today. Every conversation starts from scratch. We can hand them a sheet that pre-loads context.
Framing
This is a learn-fast pilot, not a launch. Goal is to validate cohort quality, EDS signal quality, and sales feedback before scaling.

Step 5 — AI reasoning (P1 / P2 / P3)

This node turns KEEP rows into sales posture: what to call first, what to use if engaged, and what not to lead with.

Step 5 · Reasoning

Give Sales a posture and a pitch.

Every KEEP row gets a tier, a short internal reason, and a customer-facing line. The AI only uses the intelligence row.

Input

filtered rows
Work
Rank + write
tier, reason, pitch
Output

How this helps
Reps know how hard to push and have usable language before the call starts.

Sales decision ladder

P1
Call first
Strong fit, clear story, defensible price.
webhook.link → webhook.live
Exact-match, cheap, on-theme for a real-time product.
P2
Use if engaged
Good defensive or expansion angle, but not urgent.
invariant.club → invr.club
Cheap short-form defensive add for an active dev tools brand.
P3
Do not lead
Weak fit, niche TLD, high price, or speculative angle.
logistics.kiwi → eltlogistics.com
Skip unless ELT is an existing acronym.
Trust rule
No invented use cases. If the customer's site does not support the angle, the pitch says so.

Suggestion
What to sell
Sedo aftermarket domain, Buy Now price, EDS relevancy score.

Pitch or skip
KEEP rows stay in play. DROP rows have a clear reason.

What we made easy for sales

The sheet removes three kinds of work from the rep before the first call happens.

Less choosing
Priority is pre-decided
KEEP/DROP removes risky rows. P1/P2/P3 tells the rep what deserves attention first.
Less explaining
Context is visible
The row shows whether the customer's domain is live, parked, established, or speculative.
Less writing
Pitch is ready
Every KEEP row has a short customer-facing line the rep can read or adapt.
